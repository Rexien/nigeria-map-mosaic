# Data retention

NIAC Live does not request email addresses or employee numbers. It stores an alias, opaque participant token hash, recovery-code hash, answers/timing, scores, lens submissions and operational audit events.

Recommended policy, subject to SCiN privacy approval:

- Raw participant answers and recovery credentials: delete 30 days after final result reconciliation.
- Lens submissions approved for event communications: retain only with the communications owner’s documented purpose; otherwise delete after 30 days.
- Aggregated anonymous results and admin audit logs: retain up to 12 months for event reporting and incident review.
- Rehearsal identities/answers: delete immediately after the final rehearsal.

Limit database and Netlify access to the event team, encrypt backups, avoid copying raw exports into personal storage, and document any legal hold. The organiser must approve the final schedule and participant privacy notice before launch.
