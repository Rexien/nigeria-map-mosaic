## 1. Obudu Mountain Resort Duplication

### Identified Conflict
* **Passport Trivia (Day 1, Question 11):**
  * *Question:* "Which Nigerian mountain resort in Cross River State features a famous cable car and canopy walkway?"
  * *Options:* Obudu Mountain Resort (Correct), Yankari Game Reserve, Kajuru Castle, Ikogosi Warm Springs.
* **Decode the State (Round 5: Cross River, Clue 2):**
  * *Clue Text:* "Home to the Obudu Mountain Resort, where a canopy walkway and high-altitude cable car traverse the rainforest hills."
  * *Associated Media:* `/assets/decode/14.jpg` (Obudu mountain plateau).

### Impact & Audience Experience Risk
If participants play both Day 1 Passport Trivia and Decode the State Round 5, the identical landmark, cable car, and canopy walkway clues create a direct answer giveaway, reducing the competitive integrity and discovery delight of the live event.

### Recommended Editorial Resolutions
* **Option A (Recommended — Swap Passport Q11):**  
  Keep Decode Round 5 intact and update Passport Q11 to celebrate Cross River's **Agbokim Waterfalls** or **Ikom Monoliths**:
  * *Draft Question:* "Which scenic waterfall in Cross River State is famous for seven cascading streams tumbling over a lush rainforest cliff into a tranquil pool?"
  * *Options:* Agbokim Waterfalls (Correct), Gurara Waterfalls, Farin Ruwa Falls, Erin-Ijesha Waterfalls.
  * *Explanation:* "Agbokim Waterfalls consists of seven distinct streams flowing over steep terraces near the Cameroon border."
* **Option B (Update Decode Clue 2):**  
  Retain Passport Q11 and update Decode Round 5 Clue 2 to focus on Cross River's biodiversity heritage:
  * *Draft Clue:* "Home to the Afi Mountain Wildlife Sanctuary, a critical highland forest reserve dedicated to protecting endangered drill monkeys and Cross River gorillas."

## 2. Kofar Mata Dyeing Pits Duplication

### Identified Conflict
* **Passport Trivia (Day 2, Question 12 / Question 24):**
  * *Question:* "The ancient Kofar Mata dyeing pits in Kano are world-famous for producing which traditional Nigerian craft?"
  * *Options:* Indigo dyeing (Correct), Bronze casting, Leather tanning, Wood carving.
* **Decode the State (Round 2: Kano, Clue 3):**
  * *Clue Text:* "The ancient Kofar Mata dyeing pits in its historic walled capital have produced indigo-dyed cloth using earthenware vats for over five centuries."
  * *Associated Media:* `/assets/decode/06.jpg` (Workers at Kano Dye pit).

### Impact & Audience Experience Risk
Both questions reference the exact same historic site (Kofar Mata) and the exact same artisanal craft (indigo dyeing in earthenware pits). Players who answered one question will immediately know the answer to the other.

### Recommended Editorial Resolutions
* **Option A (Recommended — Swap Passport Q24):**  
  Preserve the Kofar Mata clue in Decode the State and update Passport Q24 to celebrate **Gidan Rumfa** (the Emir's Palace) or **Kurmi Market**:
  * *Draft Question:* "Which historic Kano landmark, built in the fifteenth century by Muhammadu Rumfa, is recognized as one of West Africa's oldest continuously inhabited royal palaces?"
  * *Options:* Gidan Rumfa (Correct), Gidan Makama, Lugard Hall, Mapo Hall.
  * *Explanation:* "Gidan Rumfa has served as the palace of the Emir of Kano since 1483, featuring iconic Hausa earthen architecture."
* **Option B (Update Decode Clue 3):**  
  Retain Passport Q24 and modify Decode Round 2 Clue 3 to highlight Kano's historic trans-Saharan trading market:
  * *Draft Clue:* "Its historic center houses Kurmi Market, established in the fifteenth century as one of the principal trading hubs for trans-Saharan camel caravans."
